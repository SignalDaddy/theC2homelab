### What This Site is For
I am a **Network Engineer** and communications officer in the U.S. Army. I also have skills sets in the realm of Cloud Systems Engineering, DevOps, and Cybersecurity. I specialize in bridging the gap between military network operations and modern cloud-native infrastructure. "The C2 Homelab" is a blog-style website I built using Hugo and host over AWS. Here I can document my homelab experiments with virtualization, self-hosting, automation, A.I., cybersecurity, and other technical concepts that interest me in the moment. In short, it's my passion project of projects 

On a deeper level this is where I can also write in my blog not only to document the progression of my lab experiments, but also my mindset. I can reflect over my writing and documentation to find my problem-solving andtroublshooting mantality in the moment v years down the line.

Feel free to look around and see what I've been doing in here. If you are also be a homelaber, or tech entusiast, engineer, maker, military personnel, or human being who thinks you have something in common with me and would like to collaberate on a project or idea please feel free to contact me through one of my socials I've post here.
